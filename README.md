# Demo name: Flame Jumper. AKA - Adventures in the Age of Fire 

**Game Description:**

Welcome to a world where nature has turned against mankind! In this thrilling browser game, you control a brave person, trying to escape the apocalypse.

Your mission is to flee from the end of the world by jumping over fiery meteors that fall from the sky like the wrath of heaven itself and collect points. Each jump is a step towards salvation, each obstacle a test of your agility and reaction.

**Game Features:**
- **Unique Design:** Inspired by the famous dinosaur from Google Chrome's offline mode, but with a new, apocalyptic twist.
- **Dynamic Obstacles:** Fiery meteors falling from nowhere demand instant decision-making.
- **Survival Challenge:** How long can you last in this blazing world?

**Controls:**
- Jump: Spacebar or mouse click.

Play to find out how far you can run from the flames of doomsday. Courage, reflexes, and a bit of luck are what you need for salvation!
